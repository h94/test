## 摘要

本文档记录了为Vbet站点（SC）新增的黄牌、角球等24种特殊玩法（PlayMode）的结果计算实现任务，同时列出了46种无需额外测试但已支持的玩法。所有要求的测试玩法均已完成（标记Done），但有两个PlayMode（T1 Team Total Goals、T2 Team Total Goals）明确标注“没有做”。该文档为PriceCenter在结果结算环节需要覆盖的Vbet专属玩法提供了清单，对AI开发或维护相关结算逻辑时有参考价值。

---

## 關鍵業務規則

- - PriceCenter在比赛Final且收到resultinfo后，必须为Vbet站点的以下PlayMode计算所有odd的result（win/loss等）：YellowCardHalfHA, YellowCardHalfHA（上半场独赢）, T1YellowCardHalfOU, T2YellowCardHalfOU, T1YellowCard2ndHalfOU, T2YellowCard2ndHalfOU, A Red Card in the Match, YellowCardDouble Chance, T1YellowCardOU, T2YellowCardOU, YellowCardOU, YellowCardHA, YellowCardHA（全场独赢）, Corner2ndHalfOU, T1HalfCornerOU, T2HalfCornerOU, T12ndHalfCornerOU, T22ndHalfCornerOU, T1CornerOU, T2CornerOU, 3wayCornerOU, Corners Double Chance, Half With The Most Corners, CornerHA（见“需测试新玩法”表格）。
- PriceCenter对于Vbet站点的以下PlayMode也已支持结果计算（视为已完成，无需额外测试）：Draw No Bet, T1 No Bet, T2 No Bet, 3wayHA, 3wayOU, Goals In Both Halves, T1 To Score, T2 To Score, 1st Half Or Match Result, T1 Score And Match Result, T2 Score And Match Result, T1 No Bet(FC), T2 No Bet(FC), Outcome And Both To Score, Double Chance And Both To Score, Outcome And Total Goals 1.5/2.5/3.5/4.5, OutcomeorTotalGoals, Outcome And Total Goals (Exact), OutcomeorCorrectScore, Total Goals (Exact), T1 Correct Score, T2 Correct Score, Total Scores (Extended Bands), T1 Win By Exact Goal, T2 Win By Exact Goal, T1 Will Win and Score Exact Goal, T2 Will Win and Score Exact Goal, Anytime Correct Score, HalfDouble Chance, 1st Outcome And Both Teams To Score, Half Total Score, HalfT1 Team Total Score, HalfT2 Team Total Score, Half Total Goals, T1 Half Correct Score, T2 Half Correct Score, 2nd HalfBoth Teams To Score, 2nd Outcome And Both Teams To Score, 2nd HalfCorrect Score, First Half/Second Half Result, T1 Score in Both Halves, T2 Score in Both Halves, Headed Goals Total（见“不需要测试”表格）。
- 两个PlayMode：T1 Team Total Goals和T2 Team Total Goals明确标记为“没有做”，需人工确认当前是否已实现或仍有缺失。
